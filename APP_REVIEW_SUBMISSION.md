## 1. App purpose (paste into "Describe your app")

> "AI Customer Service Bot" is a customer-support automation tool. A business (Instagram professional account owner) connects their Instagram account with a single click using Instagram Business Login. Once connected, the service can read incoming Direct Messages from customers and send automated replies generated by an AI assistant (e.g., order status, hours of operation, FAQ answers, or hand-offs to a human agent). The app uses `instagram_business_basic` to identify the connected professional account and `instagram_business_manage_messages` to send and receive Direct Messages on behalf of the business.

---

## 2. Human Agent (Human Factor) description

Meta requires you to describe how a human is in the loop / can take over.

> **Human agent availability:** After an account is connected, the business owner reviews every automated reply in the inbox and can edit, pause, or override any message before it is sent. A human agent can fully take over the conversation at any time from the same inbox view. No AI reply is sent without the business's account being provisioned to allow the bot; the business can disable auto-reply instantly, after which the app only receives messages and takes no sending action. Contact information for a human agent is always visible: users can reply with "REPRESENTATIVE" or "AGENT" at any time and the bot immediately stops replying and notifies the human agent.

*(If your real product routes differently, adjust the mechanism — but keep the guaranteed-human-takeover story.)*

## 5. Data deletion & deauthorization responses

Both callbacks are implemented on the Worker and verified with Meta's signed requests (HMAC-SHA256).

- **Deauthorize callback** — `https://ig-oauth-exchange.ezzx11.workers.dev/deauthorize` → returns 200; the app stores no server-side data to purge.
- **Data deletion callback** — `https://ig-oauth-exchange.ezzx11.workers.dev/data-deletion` → validates the signed request, returns:
  ```json
  {
    "url": "https://ig-oauth-exchange.ezzx11.workers.dev/deletion-status?code=XXXXXXXX",
    "confirmation_code": "XXXXXXXX"
  }
  ```
  The status page permanently confirms removal.

> **Dashboard setup:** App Dashboard → Instagram → API setup with Instagram login → Business login settings →
